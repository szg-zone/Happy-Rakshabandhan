## 💖 About

**Happy Rakshabandhan** is a playful, brutalist-styled interactive web experience built to celebrate the unbreakable bond with a sister.

It starts with a high-tech "Sister Audit Protocol"...  
and ends with a heartfelt letter, 3D Rakhi experience, and an official certificate of sisterhood! 📜✨

From interactive evidence galleries to custom photo uploading and instant link sharing, every detail is crafted to turn sweet memories into an unforgettable digital keepsake.

---

## ✨ Features

📜 **Official Sister Audit Protocol** — Terminal glitch loading sequence & interactive verification  
🏅 **Customizable Certificate** — Personalized sister name, brother name, seal & stamp  
📸 **Evidence Gallery** — Upload custom photos & captions (with compressed dual-resolution storage!)  
📊 **Brutalist Statistics** — Interactive pie charts & chaos metric breakdowns  
✉️ **Personalized Envelope Letter** — Unseal a typewriter-animated letter from the heart  
🎀 **3D Rakhi Experience** — Interactive digital Rakhi tying with Web Audio SFX & confetti explosions  
🔗 **Cross-Device Link Sharing** — Embed photos & custom text directly into a compressed URL hash payload  
📱 **Responsive & Mobile Friendly** — High-contrast brutalist design optimized for all screen sizes
